## Minimal example

```xml title="Config/ModuleConfigFiles/ImportObservations 1.00 default.xml"
<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<timeSeriesImportRun xmlns="http://www.wldelft.nl/fews">
  <import>
    <general>
      <importType>generalCsv</importType>
      <folder>$IMPORT_FOLDER$/discharge</folder>
      <fileNamePatternFilter>*.csv</fileNamePatternFilter>
      <idMapId>IdImportObservations</idMapId>
      <importTimeZone>
        <timeZoneName>GMT</timeZoneName>
      </importTimeZone>
    </general>
    <timeSeriesSet>
      <moduleInstanceId>ImportObservations</moduleInstanceId>
      <valueType>scalar</valueType>
      <parameterId>Q.obs</parameterId>
      <locationId>RiverGauge_01</locationId>
      <timeSeriesType>external historical</timeSeriesType>
      <timeStep unit="hour" multiplier="1"/>
      <readWriteMode>add originals</readWriteMode>
    </timeSeriesSet>
  </import>
</timeSeriesImportRun>
```

<Aside type="caution">
Choose the reader with **one** of `importTypeStandard`, `importType`, or
`parserClassName`. `readWriteMode` is required on the `timeSeriesSet` but ignored
by the import module. See the [task
guide](/tasks/import-timeseries/#gotchas).
</Aside>
